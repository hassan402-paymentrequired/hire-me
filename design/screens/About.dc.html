About Proxideck

Good work deserves to be found. Good customers deserve to be sure.

Proxideck connects people with the stylists, barbers, cleaners and technicians around them, and makes booking them feel as easy as ordering a ride.

Booking a pro shouldn’t take ten DMs and a prayer.

Finding someone good usually means asking around, scrolling through Instagram, sending voice notes about prices, then hoping they actually show up. Pros have it just as hard: no-shows, last-minute haggling and payments that never arrive.

We built Proxideck to fix both sides. Customers see real prices, real reviews and real availability. Pros get a booking page, a calendar and a wallet that pays them, without chasing anyone.
